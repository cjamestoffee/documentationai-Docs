StoryPage ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Chrome Extension.

**1. Data Collected** To provide our core content transformation functionality, we process the following categories of user data:

- **Website content:** We capture article text, selected text, visible transcripts, and video frames from your active tab strictly when you initiate a capture to generate a story or artwork.
- **Authentication information:** We collect the user-supplied API keys you provide to connect with third-party AI services (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, xAI Grok, and [KIE.ai](http://KIE.ai)).
- **Personally identifiable information:** If you purchase StoryPage Pro, we process the information necessary to verify your license status via our API ([api.nextstepconrad.com](http://api.nextstepconrad.com)).

**2. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services** Because StoryPage acts as a bridge between your browser and AI generators, data sharing is strictly limited to the services you choose to enable:

- **AI Providers:** When you generate a story, comic, or artwork, the specific website content you captured is sent directly to the AI provider you have actively selected and configured with your API key.
- **Payment Processing:** StoryPage Pro checkout is hosted externally on Stripe. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers or financial statements.
- We do not sell your data or share it with any third parties other than your chosen AI provider and our secure license verification server.

**3. Limited Use Disclosure** StoryPage complies fully with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy. We explicitly certify the following regarding your data:

- **Affirmative Statement of Compliance:** The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
- **Single Purpose Use:** We do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to our item's single purpose of transforming webpage and video content into illustrated stories, comics, coloring books, or storyboards.
- **Financial Protections:** We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

**4. Data Storage and Security** We collect the absolute minimum data required to operate.

- Your chosen AI provider API keys, extension settings, and generated books are stored locally within your browser.
- We do not maintain centralized databases or external servers that store your generated books or browsing content.
- All communications between the extension, your chosen AI providers, and our license verification server are transmitted via secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS).

**5. Changes to This Privacy Policy** We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

**6. Contact Us** If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at [cjamestoffee@gmail.com](mailto:cjamestoffee@gmail.com).
