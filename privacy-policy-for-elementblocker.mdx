ElementBlocker — Visual Ad & Banner Remover ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Chrome Extension.

1\. Data Collected

The extension functions locally within your browser. To provide our core ad-blocking functionality, we process the following categories of user data:

Web history: We process the URLs of the web pages you visit locally to apply the appropriate ad-blocking rules.

Website content: We access the text, images, and HTML structure of the active websites you visit to identify and remove visual ads and banners.

User activity: We may monitor local page interactions (such as scrolling or element loading) strictly to ensure dynamic ads are blocked as they appear.

2\. Limited Use Disclosure

ElementBlocker complies fully with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy. We explicitly certify the following regarding your data:

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.

We do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to our item's single purpose of blocking ads and banners.

We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

3\. Data Storage

We use your browser's local storage to save your settings and preferences. This data resides solely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any external databases.

4\. Security

We collect the absolute minimum data required to operate. Because all data processing happens locally on your machine and is not transmitted over the internet by our extension, your data remains secure within your own local environment.

5\. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
